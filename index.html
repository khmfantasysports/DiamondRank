THE THREE SCORES

Three questions. Three different answers.

The scores are designed to work together. No single number is meant to describe everything about a prospect.

OVERALL

DiamondScore

Overall fantasy prospect strength. It combines path quality and fantasy skill, with supporting evidence such as prior performance, MLB results and scouting context when available.

PATH

Opportunity

How favorable a player's historical path looks based on age, level, role and peer-relative performance.

Not MLB readiness. Not a percentile of current prospects. Not a literal probability.
SKILL

Fantasy Profile

How strong the player's fantasy-relevant underlying skills look. Hitter and pitcher traits are evaluated separately using the evidence that matters for each.

ONE BOARD ACROSS LEVELS

A-ball and Triple-A belong on the same scale.

Performance is evaluated in context. A young player succeeding in A-ball can legitimately carry a stronger Opportunity score than an older player in Triple-A. Age relative to level is meaningful development evidence, not something DiamondRank removes by creating separate level-specific score scales.

CURRENT EVIDENCE LEADS

Recent performance matters most.

DiamondRank starts with the current season. Smaller samples can be stabilized by prior evidence, historical context and bounded scouting support rather than allowing one brief stretch to completely redefine a player's profile.

How to read the numbers

DiamondRank scores use a 0.0–99.9 model scale. Higher means stronger evidence on that dimension. Scores are not guarantees, MLB-readiness grades or percentile ranks of the current prospect population.
