WHY LEVELS SHARE ONE BOARD

A young A-ball player can rank above an older Triple-A player.

That is intentional. Performance is evaluated against appropriate peers, while age relative to level is itself meaningful development evidence. DiamondRank does not create separate score scales for each minor-league level.
