Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

DiamondRank is a fantasy baseball prospect-ranking website. This policy explains what information the public site currently collects and how contact information is handled.

Information we collect

The public DiamondRank site currently does not offer user accounts, public-facing forms, analytics tracking, or advertising cookies. DiamondRank does not intentionally collect personal information through normal use of the public ranking pages.

If you contact DiamondRank by email, we receive the information you choose to send, including your email address and message.

Technical information

Hosting, database, content-delivery, and other infrastructure providers may process ordinary technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, request timestamps, and server logs as part of operating and securing their services.

How information is used

Information received through email may be used to answer questions, respond to feedback, address technical issues, or otherwise communicate about DiamondRank. Technical information may be processed as needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the site.

Sharing and selling information

DiamondRank does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate the site or email service, or disclosed when required by law.

Third-party services and links

DiamondRank relies on third-party infrastructure and may link to third-party websites. Those services have their own privacy practices and policies. DiamondRank is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party sites.

Retention

Email correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain support history, or meet legal and operational requirements.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if DiamondRank adds new features or changes how information is handled. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to diamondrank.app@gmail.com.
